# ETEN Technology Foundation

**Keeping Bible translation tools open, protected, and available.**

A program of ETEN, hosted by Mission Mutual.

The ETEN Technology Foundation is a long-term home for open-source Bible translation technology and the open-licensed data and content related to it. We hold the legal title, the accounts and the keys, so the tools you build and depend on stay open for everyone, whoever builds or maintains them next.

An open license protects the code. Someone still has to look after the project.

## What we do

- **Hold.** We safely and legally hold open-source technology that matters to Bible translation, along with the open-licensed data and content related to it, so it stays open and available.
- **List.** If you'd rather keep your project, it stays with you. We list it and help you coordinate with others working nearby.

Every held project has a named maintainer and decision-maker who keep guiding it.

## Our commitments

- Every project we hold carries an approved open license from the day we accept it, and that license is never withdrawn.
- We will not sell a project we hold or relicense it on more restrictive terms.
- If the Foundation ever closes or changes hands, the projects we hold move to another nonprofit committed to keeping them open. Licenses already granted continue.

## Contribute

Each project accepts contributions through its own repository, under that project's open license. Sign off on each commit (`git commit -s`) to confirm the work is yours to give. There's no separate agreement to sign.
